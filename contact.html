What helps

  • A sample fileAnonymised is fine. The shape matters more than the contents.
  • The volumeRows per week or per month, and how often the format changes.
  • Where it livesExcel, Google Sheets, a CRM export or an inbox.
  • Who uses the outputThe person who reads the report, not just the one who builds it.
  • The deadlineIf there is one, say it early; it changes what gets built first.
